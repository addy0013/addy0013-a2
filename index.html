Where is Home?

I was born in Iowa, USA -- random I know. After that, I spent 18 years of my life growing up in Accra, Ghana.
Ghana is amazing. There's so much peace, the people are super friendly and quite lighthearted (maybe a bit too much)
and of course the food is unforgettable.

As for Iowa, I've been back once since birth, when I was maybe 12 years old.
The highlight of that trip was the food. Fresh from the farm and SO TASTY!

Currently I live in Ottawa, Ontario and I've been here for the past 4 years.
I have loved every bit of it, although I'm still not used the cold winters.
So where is home?
